# Changelog

## 0.2.0 — 2026-10-07

- Add a frozen eight-task composition split that is disjoint from the acquisition target truth tables.
- After vocabulary acquisition, start fresh matched populations for each held-out task, freeze snippets/opcodes, and prohibit further publication or expiry during transfer measurement.
- Report separate acquisition and unseen-composition scores, paired contrasts, seed/task receipts, and phase-labeled CSV rows.
- Version receipts as tessera-study-v2 and preserve compatibility with older v1 browser ledger entries.

## 0.1.2 — 2026-10-07

- Combine exact-match accuracy with bitwise output agreement as the evolutionary training signal while retaining exact-match accuracy as the reported accuracy metric.
- Record per-epoch opcode-gate surveys, including how many candidate functions were considered and how many passed.

## 0.1.1 — 2026-10-07

- Keep retired opcode definitions available to programs that already contain them while removing those opcodes from the public grammar.
- Require ratified publications to be observed in separate single-founder lineages; multi-founder recombinations do not count toward independent rediscovery.
- Record exact training pairs and local runtime in each study receipt.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-07

- Initial local experiment on shared fragments, exact-table opcodes, and cross-task opcode commons.
